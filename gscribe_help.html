Quick start

  1. Click notes in the grid to turn them on. The sheet music above updates as you go.
  2. Press the play button (or the Space bar) to hear your groove. It repeats until you stop it.
  3. Set the speed with the BPM slider and add some swing if you like.
  4. Open the menu (the three bars at the top right) to pick a ready-made groove, set the time signature, add a title, print or share.

Tip: your groove is stored in the page address, and the address updates as you edit. Bookmarking the page, or copying its link, saves the groove.

The menu

Everything except the metronome, the player and the Edit mode / View mode switch lives in the menu at the top right.

Edit mode / View mode (top right, next to the menu) hides the editing grid so only the sheet music and the player show. The button names the mode you will switch to. A link you share remembers which mode you were in.

Editing a groove

Turning notes on and off

  • Click a note to turn it on; click it again to turn it off. A snare note turns on as an accent.
  • Right-click a note for its menu of special notes: hi-hat open, accent, crash, ride, ride bell, cow bell, stacker and click; snare accent, ghost, cross stick, buzz and flam; kick and hi-hat foot.
  • Hold Ctrl and move the mouse across notes to turn a whole run on. Hold Alt and move across them to turn them off.
  • On a touch screen there is no right-click: use Advanced Edit (see Phones & tablets).

Row labels

Click a row label (Hi-hat, Snare, Kick, the toms, Stickings) for a menu that acts on the whole measure: turn every note off or on, only the downbeats or upbeats on the hi-hat, accented, normal or ghost snares, and so on. From the same menu you can mute an instrument so you hear the groove without it. A muted row shows a red speaker icon; click it to unmute.

Stickings and toms

  • Turn them on from Display → Show stickings / Show toms.
  • Clicking a sticking cell cycles through R, L, R/L (both), a count number, then off. The Stickings row label has a menu to fill the whole measure with alternating R/L, all R, all L or counts.
  • Display → Reverse stickings R/L swaps every R and L.

Measures

The + button after the last measure adds a measure (it starts as a copy of the one before it). Once there is more than one measure, the × on a measure removes it.

Starting from a groove

Pick a groove in Grooves and edit it, or choose Empty 16th note groove to start from nothing. Edit → Clear all empties every note of the current groove. Choosing a groove replaces what is on screen, so use Undo if you did not mean to.

Undo and redo

Edit → Undo and Redo, or Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y. Undo steps back through the changes you made to the groove one at a time, including changes to the time signature and subdivision (pressing Done in Groove setup counts as one change).

Title, author and comment

Open Groove setup to type a title, an author and a comment; they print above the music. Each box has a × to clear it.

Legend

Display → Show legend adds a key above the music that names each notation symbol, which is useful when you share or print a groove that uses special notes. Hide legend removes it.

Playing

  • The play/pause button, or Space, starts and stops playback. The groove loops until you stop it.
  • Tempo is set with the BPM slider (30–300) or by typing a number in the BPM box. The ← and → keys nudge it down and up by 1 BPM.
  • Swing sets how much the offbeats are delayed. See Swing.
  • You can keep editing while the groove plays and hear the change as you make it.
  • The drum sounds load when the page opens, so if you press play very quickly there can be a short pause before you hear anything.

Metronome

The metronome is on the top bar. OFF, 4th, 8th and 16th choose how often it clicks while the groove plays. The selected one is green.

SettingWhat you hear
4thA click on every beat, with a distinct sound on the 1.
8thThe above, plus a click on every “and”. In a triplet subdivision it clicks on eighth-note triplets.
16thThe above, plus a quieter click on every 16th note. In a triplet subdivision it clicks on sixteenth-note triplets.

Options

The Options button opens more metronome settings. It is highlighted while any of them is on.

OptionWhat it does
Metronome onlyPlays just the metronome and mutes the groove when you press play. Turning it on with the metronome off switches the metronome to 4th. Turning the metronome off also turns this off.
Auto speed upGradually raises the tempo while you play. In the dialog, set Amount in BPM and Interval in minutes: the tempo rises by that many BPM every interval. With Keep increasing after the first interval ticked it carries on indefinitely; with it unticked it stops once it has gone up by the amount once. Cancel leaves the option off.
Count it inWhen you press play, plays one measure of clicks (a distinct sound on the 1) before the groove starts.
Offset clickMoves the metronome’s accent so the pattern starts on the “e”, the “&” or the “a” instead of the 1 (in triplets, the second or third note of the beat). Rotate through moves it to the next position each time the groove loops. Choose Start on “1” to go back to normal.

Swing

The SWING slider (0–50%, in steps of 5) adds swing to playback. It does not change the printed music. To swing a groove, the first note of each pair is lengthened and the second is shortened, so the offbeats land late.

Swing works on 8th, 16th and 32nd note grooves. It shows N/A for triplet subdivisions, which already have a swing feel built in.

There is no “correct” amount of swing. It varies a great deal between players and styles, so try different settings until it feels right. Wikipedia has a good article on the types of swing (scroll to the “Types” section).

Swing settingFeelRatio of the pair
20%Light3 : 2
30–35%Medium, the classic triplet swing feel (a triplet quarter followed by a triplet eighth)about 2 : 1
50%Heavy, a dotted eighth followed by a sixteenth3 : 1

Tip: the slider moves in steps of 5, so there is no exact 33%. 30% and 35% either side of it are the closest to a true triplet feel (about 1.9 : 1 and 2.1 : 1).

Time signature & subdivision

Both are in Groove setup. Change them, then press Done.

  • Time signature: the top number goes from 2 to 15, the bottom number is 4, 8 or 16.
  • Subdivision is how finely each measure is divided into notes: 1/8 notes, 1/16 notes, 1/32 notes, 1/8 triplets or 1/16 triplets.
  • Mixed division lets one measure combine triplets and straight notes. Use every 3rd note for 16ths and every 6th note for 8th notes.
  • Some combinations are not available, and those options are greyed out. 1/8 notes are unavailable in time signatures where they do not divide a measure evenly, and the triplet subdivisions only work when the bottom number is 4.

Careful: when a new time signature or subdivision has fewer notes per measure than the old one, notes that no longer fit are dropped. If that happens, Undo brings them back.

Permutations

A permutation exercise plays every combination of one limb against a fixed pattern, so you can practice them one after another. Permutations work in 4/4 time only; in other time signatures the Permutations menu item says so.

Open Permutations and choose:

  • Kick Permutation: you write the hi-hat and snare; GrooveScribe steps the kick drum through the combinations. The kick row is hidden because it is generated for you. Any hi-hat foot notes you wrote are kept.
  • Snare Permutation: you write the hi-hat and kick; GrooveScribe steps the snare through the combinations. The snare row is hidden.
  • None: back to normal editing.

Turning a permutation on closes all measures except the first. Undo restores them.

Permutation options

A panel of checkboxes appears above the music. Each section can be turned on or off, and each section has boxes for which beat it starts on (1, e, &, a; in triplets 1, &, a).

  • Ostinato: just your own pattern, with no permutation.
  • Singles, Doubles, Triples, Downbeats/Upbeats, Quads: the different permutation sections. Downbeats/Upbeats and Quads are not available in triplet subdivisions.
  • Use Accent Grid (snare): plays all the 16th notes on the snare and accents the permutation pattern.
  • Simplify multiple kicks (kick): skips the kick on the “1” and “1e” when it directly follows a kick on the “4a” of the previous measure. This usually flows better when you play the whole pattern through.

Saving & sharing

Saving a groove

GrooveScribe keeps no account or server copy. The whole groove is encoded in the page address and the address updates as you edit, so bookmarking the page saves it, and so does pasting the link into a note or email.

Share link

Share & Export → Share link opens a box with the link to your groove.

  • Copy puts the link on your clipboard.
  • Short URL asks for a shorter link that is easier to paste into messages. If it cannot make one, it unticks itself and the full link is used.
  • Embed Code gives a snippet of HTML you can paste into your own web page to show the groove there with the player.
  • The buttons beside Copy share the link by email and on social sites.

Anyone who opens the link sees your groove in any web browser, with nothing to install.

Printing and exporting

  • Print prints only the sheet music.
  • Download SVG images and Download PNG images save the sheet music as pictures, SVG for a sharp result at any size and PNG for a general-purpose picture.
  • Download MIDI file saves the groove as a standard MIDI file you can open in other music software.

Phones & tablets

  • Touch screens have no right-click, so GrooveScribe has Advanced Edit. When it is on, tapping a note opens the same menu of special notes that right-click gives on a computer. It is in Edit and shows a green check when it is on.
  • On a phone Advanced Edit starts switched on. Switch it off in the same menu if you would rather tap to turn notes on and off.
  • The menu works the same way on every screen size. On a narrow screen its submenus may open over the main menu rather than beside it; tap outside to close them.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the sound plays even when the silent switch is on.

Troubleshooting

  • No sound. Check the device volume, and press play once: browsers do not allow audio until you have interacted with the page. If Metronome only is on, the groove itself is muted. Individual rows can also be muted from their row label menu.
  • The Permutations menu is grey. Permutations need the 4/4 time signature.
  • I lost some notes. Changing the time signature or subdivision can drop notes that no longer fit. Use Undo.
  • Swing says N/A. Swing is not used for triplet subdivisions.
  • The Short URL box unticked itself. The shortening service could not be reached; copy the full link instead.
